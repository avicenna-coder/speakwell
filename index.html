Dear friend,

Picture this;

You are in a meeting.

A question is thrown to the room.

You know the answer.

You have actually known it for a while.

But before you open your mouth, your brain starts editing. Is it “I have been” or “I was”? Will this sound too simple? What if I say it wrongly and people notice?

By the time you have built the perfect sentence, someone else has spoken.

The moment is gone.

You nod, stay quiet and tell yourself, “Next time.”

Next time comes. It happens again.

If that is you, I want you to hear this first: it is not because you are unintelligent.

In my years of teaching, I have encountered brilliant engineers, nurses, bankers, entrepreneurs, graduates and other professionals who share this frustration. They have ideas, qualifications and experience.

What they lack is confidence that their English will carry those ideas effectively.

Confidence in English is not simply a personality trait. It is a skill that can be developed through the right instruction, deliberate practice and feedback.

That is exactly what this 8-week cohort is designed to help you do.

You have probably tried to improve.

Perhaps you have watched free lessons, read grammar books, downloaded apps or told yourself to “just speak more.”

These efforts can help, but they may not solve the problem on their own.

Here is why:

You learned grammar to pass exams, not necessarily to communicate. School may have taught you rules to identify in a test without showing you how to use them naturally in a live conversation.

You may not have had a safe place to practise out loud. Watching a lesson can help you recognise correct English. Speaking, receiving feedback and trying again help you become better at producing it.

You may never have followed a structured system. Random tips and isolated lessons can leave gaps. A clear progression, practised consistently with feedback, gives you a path to follow.

The problem is not that you cannot improve. You may simply need a more practical and structured approach.

INTRODUCING: THE SPEAK WITH CONFIDENCE COHORT

This is a live, 8-week small-group virtual programme designed to help you move from hesitant and over-careful to clearer, more structured and more confident communication.

What you will learn;

  1. WEEK 1:

    BUILD GRAMMATICALLY CORRECT SENTENCES.

    Master the foundations of English grammar, sentence construction, parts of speech, phrases and clauses.

    Learn to construct complete, meaningful sentences and express your thoughts correctly.

  2. WEEK 2:

    MASTER ENGLISH TENSES

    Learn to communicate accurately about past experiences, present activities and future intentions.

    Understand the different tenses and address common tense-related errors.

  3. WEEK 3:

    CONCORD, PRONOUNS AND GRAMMATICAL ACCURACY

    Master subject–verb agreement, pronoun usage and other essential grammatical rules.

    Identify and correct common errors that affect the accuracy of your spoken English.

  4. WEEK 4:

    PREPOSITIONS, ARTICLES AND OTHER FUNCTIONAL WORDS

    Understand how to use prepositions, articles, conjunctions, determiners and quantifiers correctly.

    Learn to express relationships between ideas with greater precision.

  5. WEEK 5:

    ADVANCED GRAMMAR FOR EFFECTIVE EXPRESSION

    Explore conditional sentences, active and passive voice, reported speech, gerunds, infinitives, modals and other important grammatical structures.

    Learn to construct more sophisticated sentences without common grammatical errors.

  6. WEEK 6:

    EXPRESSING YOUR THOUGHTS CLEARLY AND FLUENTLY

    Learn how to organise your thoughts, manage hesitation, retrieve words more readily and express ideas without rambling.

    Develop your ability to speak spontaneously and communicate with clarity.

  7. WEEK 7:

    EFFECTIVE CONVERSATIONS AND PUBLIC SPEAKING

    Learn how to initiate and sustain conversations, ask thoughtful questions, express opinions, disagree respectfully, tell compelling stories and deliver structured speeches and presentations.

  8. WEEK 8:

    PRONUNCIATION, DELIVERY AND SPEAKING WITH CONFIDENCE

    Improve pronunciation, intonation, sentence stress, speaking pace and voice projection.

    Learn to use pauses effectively, manage nervousness and communicate with greater confidence.

    Conclude with a speaking showcase to demonstrate your progress.
